About GfG Campus Club

Our Mission

The GeeksforGeeks RIT Campus Club is a vibrant community of passionate learners and developers. Our mission is to bridge the gap between academic learning and industry demands by fostering a culture of continuous learning, coding proficiency, and technological innovation.

We aim to provide a platform where students can collaborate, compete, and grow together through workshops, hackathons, and rigorous coding challenges.
